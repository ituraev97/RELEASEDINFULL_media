## 2. Marchetti / taxi / cost
- Victor Marchetti (ex-special assistant to DCI; book "The Agency", p.208 per NSArchive blog) : cat wired, tail antenna, killed by taxi in park test. https://unredacted.com/2010/03/05/document-friday-acoustic-kitty/ (blog presents as his account, unverified). NSArchive description: "the cat was dead!" https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB438/. Also Adam Curtis 1995 BBC interview (PopSci).
- Wisconsin Avenue / Soviet compound: NOT found in any opened source. Wikipedia (opened) says: released near a park outside the Soviet embassy in DC, hit by taxi (attributed to Marchetti). Treat location specifics as UNVERIFIED. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_Kitty
- Contradiction: Robert Wallace (ex-OTS director), per Wikipedia 2013 citation: "the equipment was taken out of the cat; the cat was re-sewn for a second time, and lived a long and happy life afterwards." PopSci: Bob Bailey (animal trainer) disputes Marchetti, calls it "a serious project". CIA's own page: cat "not harmed during implantation or removal", calls the death story a joke by a former official (https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/) - my paraphrase via fetch tool; re-check wording.
- $20 million: Wikipedia attributes to Marchetti (Herald Sun 2001; Curtis). Smithsonian says "estimates ~$20 million" itself. WETA cites $15 million (search result only, NOT OPENED). NOT documented in any CIA record. Mark as claim/estimate.

## 3. CIA's own account
- https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/ : began 1964 (mic+transmitter cat); program ended 1967 without operational use; "The technology worked, but the problem was something all cat owners are quite familiar with." Pigeon: "CIA sent out pigeons with secret cameras to gather intelligence during the Cold War." Insectothopter: "disguised as an everyday dragon fly", 1970s, "Unfortunately, our little dragonfly proved not to be operational" (crosswinds). Charlie and Charlene robotic catfish (Office of Advanced Technologies and Programs, water samples). No ravens. Also bumblebee, tiger-scat seismic device, rats as dead drops, K9s. No photo credits on page.
- Other (not CIA): NPR (https://www.npr.org/transcripts/233354052) mentions "Squab Squad" pigeons and a raven program (Wallace/Vanderbilt-type secondary sourcing; not CIA-origin). Smithsonian (https://www.smithsonianmag.com/history/the-cias-most-highly-trained-spies-werent-even-human-20149/?page=3): Bailey: "We found that we could condition the cat to listen to voices"; wire from inner ear to battery cluster in rib cage; target an Asian head of state (Wallace/Melton). Secondary.
- CIA-origin official-record list of "ravens": none found.
