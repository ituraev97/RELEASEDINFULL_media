How did Jeffrey Epstein die, and how was one of the most notorious inmates in federal custody left unchecked for almost eight hours? We read the Justice Department Inspector General's 128-page report on the Epstein jail death at the Metropolitan Correctional Center, the night of August 9-10, 2019.

Here is what the official record shows: the cellmate who was never replaced, the cameras that stopped recording, the rounds that were signed for but never walked, the missing minute of jail video, and what the FBI and the Inspector General concluded.
