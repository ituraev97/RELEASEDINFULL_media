Memo page numbers below are scan numbers (p-01 … p-15).

**Key findings (details in the table):**
1. **The October 1997 ARRB letter is about a different memo.** It concerns a CJCS memo to the SecDef dated **8 August 1962**, not the 13 March memo. L60 and L62 present it as if it were the Northwoods release.
2. **No primary source found here dates the release of the 13 March memo to 1997.** NSArchive says only "in the 1990s". "Thirty-five years" (L70) is unsupported.
3. **The JCS biography does not tie Lemnitzer's non-reappointment to the Bay of Pigs** (L55). It says Kennedy judged him "not sufficiently innovative" for Third World warfare.
4. **Annex course no. 1 is not Guantanamo** (L28). It is a cover-and-deception plan. Guantanamo is no. 2.
5. **Several b-roll labels mislead by juxtaposition:**
   - The "records move" clip is President Obama's materials leaving the National Archives in 2016, not JFK records.
   - The QF-16 clip shows a piloted flight, not an uncrewed one.
   - The Coast Guard cutter Bear clip is a homecoming in Portsmouth, Va.
6. **Two recreated documents have invented titles** ("Reference", "Meeting with the President").
7. **Two "QUOTED VERBATIM" lists contain trimmed or altered wording.**
8. **The L23 quote is mis-sourced.** It comes from the Appendix to Enclosure A (a DRAFT memo), not the annex.
