On March 13, 1962, the Joint Chiefs of Staff sent the Secretary of Defense a top-secret memo: "Justification for US Military Intervention in Cuba." Its annex listed pretexts — a staged attack on Guantanamo, a U.S. ship blown up and blamed on Cuba, a fake airliner shoot-down, a terror campaign in Florida. We read the declassified document itself: who asked for it, what it proposed, what the president said three days later, and how it became public through the JFK Records Act. No pretext in it was carried out, according to the released record.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
