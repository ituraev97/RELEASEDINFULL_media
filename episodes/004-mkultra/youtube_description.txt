In January 1973, the CIA destroyed the records of MKUltra, its secret program to research drugs and other ways to influence human behavior. In 1977, a search prompted by Freedom of Information Act requests turned up seven boxes the destruction had missed. We read the documents: the 1953 approval memo, the CIA Inspector General's 1963 report, the Senate's 1977 hearing and the Church Committee's findings. They cover LSD tests on people who never knew, the death of Army scientist Frank Olson, and what the surviving finance files named.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
