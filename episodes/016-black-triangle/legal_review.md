## Overall verdict

The script is careful. Every spoken claim about the 2026 sighting traces to the AARO MFR or the FD-302. The narration names no witness, no minor and no living private person. Before publishing it still needs **16 FIXes**. Most are small. The most important:

1. **The "use NASA for a cover" line (L73, L74, TikTok s4).** The script presents this as fact, but the memo records it only as what Frutkin "seems to feel".
2. **On-screen private names in the Sweden crops (sw_robot, sw_meteor).**
3. **Contractor logo and cover photo in `ranch_cover.jpg`.**
4. **"The ranch's own dogs" (L79, L82).** The record says the dogs were on a redacted property, not the ranch.
5. **Packaging.** Three titles or thumbnail lines attribute claims to "the FBI" or "the Government" that those records do not make.
