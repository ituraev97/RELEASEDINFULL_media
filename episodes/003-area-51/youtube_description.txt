In 1998 the CIA published its secret history of the U-2 spy plane with whole pages blacked out. In 2013, a less-redacted version came back under the Freedom of Information Act, with a map and a name: Area 51. We read the CIA's own files: how a dry lakebed in Nevada was picked from the air in 1955, why the base was named for a map designation, why the agency's historians tied more than half of the UFO reports of the late 1950s and 1960s to its spy planes, and what came next with the A-12 OXCART.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
