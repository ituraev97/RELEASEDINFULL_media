On October 14, 1962, an Air Force U-2 spent about six minutes over western Cuba and came back with 928 photographs. Two days later, President Kennedy was looking at Soviet ballistic missile sites. We read the declassified record: John McCone's warning cables, the September 1962 intelligence estimate that called missiles in Cuba "incompatible with Soviet practice," the CIA memo and range map of October 16, the White House transcripts, the quarantine, the U.N. showdown — and what the cameras never saw: the nuclear warheads, revealed only decades later.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
