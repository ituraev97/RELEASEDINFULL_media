## 1. The three videos

### FLIR1 ("Tic Tac"), 14 November 2004
- **Date and setting:** Recorded 14 Nov 2004 during USS Nimitz Carrier Strike Group training off Southern California, about 100 miles southwest of San Diego. https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony
- **DoD's own description:** A DoD public-affairs card (released via FOIA 21-F-1458) says the November 2004 "FLIR" video came from aircraft from USS Nimitz. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf
- **Aircraft and crew:** Filmed by a second-wave F/A-18F crew, according to Fravor's written House statement. The Black Vault identifies the aircrew as Lt. Cmdr. Chad Underwood's, using the ATFLIR targeting pod. https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/David-Fravor-Statement-for-House-Oversight-Committee.pdf ; https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-the-tic-tac-incident-november-14-2004/
- **What Underwood says he saw:** According to Underwood (NY Magazine interview, 2019, as summarized by Wikipedia), he never saw the object with his own eyes, only on the sensor. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_UFO_videos
- **Audio:** The released clip has no audio. https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-the-tic-tac-incident-november-14-2004/
- **Length:** About 1 min 16 s per The Black Vault. Fravor's statement calls it a "roughly 90-second video." [VERIFY against the NAVAIR file "1 - FLIR.mp4".] https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-the-tic-tac-incident-november-14-2004/
- **Earlier circulation:** The DoD card states a copy was posted publicly in 2007. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf

### GIMBAL and GOFAST, January 2015
- **Carrier and region:** USS Theodore Roosevelt Carrier Strike Group, off the U.S. East Coast. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_UFO_videos
- **Recording date:** According to The Black Vault, the Navy told John Greenewald that both GIMBAL and GOFAST were recorded on **21 January 2015**. The 2020 DoD statement says only "January 2015." https://sofrep.com/news/us-navy-reveals-the-truth-about-two-infamous-ufo-videos/
- **Squadron:** According to The New York Times (2019), the pilots were from VFA-11 "Red Rippers," flying Super Hornets from Roosevelt during training from Virginia to Florida. https://www.sbs.com.au/news/wow-what-is-that-us-navy-pilots-report-unexplained-flying-objects
- **Context:** Roosevelt was in its COMPTUEX pre-deployment exercise in January 2015. A DVIDS photo shows a VFA-11 F/A-18F launching on 19 Jan 2015. https://www.dvidshub.net/image/1746292/comptuex
- **GOFAST on-screen readouts:** The ATFLIR display shows sensor azimuth and elevation, field of view, aircraft altitude and speed, and range (RNG). Analysts used these readouts: the jet was at about 25,000 ft and showed 259 knots calibrated airspeed. https://www.metabunk.org/posts/220136/
- **GOFAST length:** 34 seconds. AARO analyzed "the publicly available 34-second" video. https://www.dailygrail.com/2025/02/not-so-gofast-uap-government-office-presents-analysis-of-navy-video/
- **GIMBAL length:** About 34–38 s (container 34.467 s; ExifTool reads 0:37–0:38). https://www.metabunk.org/goto/post?id=232095
- **GIMBAL audio:** Crew remarks include "a whole fleet" (referring to the SA display) and that the object is rotating. [VERIFY exact wording against the file before quoting.] https://www.uapedia.ai/wiki/the-roosevelt-encounters-how-gimbal-and-gofast-rewrote-the-uap-conversation/

## 2. How they became public
- **2007:** A copy of the 2004 FLIR video was posted publicly. A DoD card says the video had been widely shared aboard ship. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf
- **4 Oct 2017:** Luis Elizondo resigned from DoD. https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-black-vaults-post-2017-uap-ufo-timeline-project/
- **11 Oct 2017:** To The Stars Academy held its launch press conference. https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-black-vaults-post-2017-uap-ufo-timeline-project/
- **16 Dec 2017:** The NYT (Cooper, Blumenthal, Kean) and Politico revealed the Pentagon program. The NYT published the FLIR and GIMBAL videos, and TTSA put them out the same day.
  - Wikipedia says the Washington Post published GOFAST. TTSA released GOFAST in March 2018, per Metabunk's thread start date.
  - Wikipedia credits Christopher Mellon with providing the videos to the press.
  - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_UFO_videos ; https://seclists.org/interesting-people/2017/Dec/57 ; https://www.metabunk.org/posts/220136/
- **DoD's later position:** A DoD card says the two 2015 videos reached the NYT through an "unauthorized release." It says the Navy had no information on how they got into circulation. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf
- **September 2019 Navy confirmation:** Navy spokesman Joseph Gradisher said the three videos were "copies of official Navy footage." He said the Navy characterized the phenomena "as unidentified." He also said the videos show incursions into Navy training ranges. https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/navy-confirms-videos-did-capture-ufo-sightings-it-calls-them-n1056201 ; https://41nbc.com/2019/09/19/yep-those-are-ufos-navy-says-about-3-videos-of-strange-sightings/
- **The "UAP" label:** The Black Vault first reported Gradisher's wording. Gradisher said the Navy uses "Unidentified Aerial Phenomena" as a basic descriptor for unidentified objects in training-range airspace. https://www.foxla.com/news/ufo-videos-are-footage-of-real-unidentified-objects-us-navy-acknowledges
- **Gradisher on TTSA:** He disputed TTSA's claim that the videos had been approved for public release. https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/navy-confirms-videos-did-capture-ufo-sightings-it-calls-them-n1056201
- **27 April 2020 official release:** The statement is titled "Statement by the Department of Defense on the Release of Historical Navy Videos."
  - It says DoD "has authorized the release of three unclassified Navy videos," taken in November 2004 and January 2015.
  - It notes the videos had circulated after unauthorized releases in 2007 and 2017.
  - Sources: https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/navy-officially-releases-ufo-uap-videos-calls-them-historical/ ; https://christophermellon.net/post/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos
- **Reason given:** DoD said it was releasing them to "clear up any misconceptions by the public" about whether the footage was real, or whether there was more to it. https://www.theregister.com/2020/04/27/dod_ufo_video/
- **Other key lines in the statement:**
  - The release "does not reveal any sensitive capabilities or systems."
  - It does not affect ongoing investigations.
  - The phenomena "remain characterized as 'unidentified.'"
  - Source: https://www.theregister.com/2020/04/27/dod_ufo_video/ ; https://www.twz.com/33179/navy-officially-releases-infamous-ufo-videos
  - Official URL as cited by Wikipedia: https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/ [VERIFY. It was previously at defense.gov with the same article ID 2165713.]
- **Signer:** Wikipedia attributes the statement to Pentagon spokesperson Sue Gough. [VERIFY] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_UFO_videos
- **2025 FOIA release:** DoD later released 264 pages of Gough emails about GIMBAL, FLIR1 and GO FAST (FOIA 20-F-1095, partly redacted), published June 2025. https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/emails-from-pentagon-spokesperson-uncovered-on-gimbal-flir1-and-gofast-ufo-videos/
- **Withheld footage:** In September 2022, the Navy confirmed it holds more UAP footage but refused to release it. A Navy FOIA official said release "will harm national security." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentagon_UFO_videos

### ODNI Preliminary Assessment, 25 June 2021
- **Scope:** 144 reports from USG sources, covering Nov 2004 to Mar 2021. https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/702 Documents/declassified/Prelimary-Assessment-UAP-20210625.pdf
- **Explained:** Only one was identified with high confidence, as a "large, deflating balloon." https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/702 Documents/declassified/Prelimary-Assessment-UAP-20210625.pdf
- **Other numbers:**
  - 80 reports involved multiple sensors.
  - 18 incidents, in 21 reports, showed unusual movement patterns or flight characteristics.
  - 11 reports were near-misses.
  - Five potential explanatory categories: airborne clutter, natural atmospheric phenomena, USG/industry programs, foreign adversary systems, and "other."
  - Source: https://www.intelligence.gov/assets/documents/702 Documents/declassified/Prelimary-Assessment-UAP-20210625.pdf
- **Alternate mirror:** https://s3.documentcloud.org/documents/20973292/prelimary-assessment-uap-20210625.pdf (via https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/office-director-national-intelligence-releases-preliminary-report-unidentified-aerial-phenomena)

### AARO Historical Record Report, Vol. 1 (delivered to Congress in early March 2024; DoD statement 8 March 2024)
- **Scope:** All USG UAP investigations since 1945, drawing on classified and unclassified archives and dozens of interviews. https://rollcall.com/2024/03/08/no-evidence-of-extraterrestrial-technology-report-finds/
- **Main finding:** No verifiable evidence that any UAP sighting was extraterrestrial technology. No evidence that the USG or private companies have, or are reverse-engineering, such technology. https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/report-on-the-historical-record-of-u-s-government-involvement-with-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-uap-volume-i/amp
- **Alleged hidden programs:** AARO says they either don't exist, are misidentified real national-security programs, or trace back to a disestablished program, a reference to AAWSAP/AATIP. https://rollcall.com/2024/03/08/no-evidence-of-extraterrestrial-technology-report-finds/
- **Navy videos:** The report does not analyze the three Navy videos (they are not mentioned in the coverage reviewed). https://justinsnead.substack.com/p/aaros-2024-historical-report-volume

### NASA UAP Independent Study Team final report (released 14 Sept 2023; chair David Spergel)
- **Report PDF:** https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf
- **GoFast altitude:** NASA put the object at about 13,000 ft, calculated from camera angle, range and jet altitude. https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf
- **GoFast speed:** About 390 m traveled in 22 s, roughly **40 mph**, which is typical wind speed at that altitude. NASA concludes the object was most likely drifting with the wind. The report says the object "need not be moving at an extraordinary velocity." https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf
- **Other videos:** The report does not discuss GIMBAL or FLIR1. https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf
- **Release date:** The 14 Sept 2023 date comes from secondary citations, because the PDF itself carries no date. https://www.nowdeclassified.com/guides/pentagon-uap-videos

### AARO and the specific videos
- **GOFAST is formally resolved.**
  - AARO's "GoFast Case Resolution" card is dated 6 Feb 2025.
  - Findings: the object was not near the water but at about 13,000 ft, moving with the wind. Its apparent speed is due to motion parallax, and its estimated true speed is **5–92 mph** depending on wind. That is consistent with a small object such as a balloon or drone.
  - AARO notes the limitations itself: the original file and metadata were lost, it analyzed the compressed public video, and full jet telemetry was not available.
  - Sources: https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/pentagon-releases-aaro-report-on-go-fast-video-analysis/ ; https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/UAP-Case-Resolution-Reports
  - Official PDF (could not be fetched here. [VERIFY]): https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/case_resolution_reports/AARO_GoFast_Case_Resolution_Card_Methodology_Final.pdf
- **Kosloski's Senate testimony:** On 19 Nov 2024, before the Senate Armed Services Emerging Threats subcommittee, AARO Director Jon Kosloski said AARO assessed "with high confidence that the object is not actually close to the water." https://www.space.com/space-exploration/search-for-life/pentagon-ufo-chief-tells-senate-very-anomalous-objects-need-careful-study-video ; https://www.foxnews.com/us/pentagon-solves-one-its-highest-profile-ufo-mysteries

Neither appears on AARO's case-resolution page. https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/UAP-Case-Resolution-Reports ; https://www.dailygrail.com/2025/02/not-so-gofast-uap-government-office-presents-analysis-of-navy-video/

## 5. Skeptical and alternative analyses (theories)
- **Mick West's initial verdicts (April 2020):**
  - FLIR1: most likely a distant plane.
  - GIMBAL: most likely a plane's IR glare, with the apparent rotation an artifact of the camera's gimbal mount.
  - GOFAST: most likely a balloon, with the apparent speed caused by parallax.
  - Source: https://www.leonarddavid.com/?p=23728
- **West's GOFAST analysis (Metabunk, from March 2018):** He estimated the object at about 13,000 ft, between the jet at 25,000 ft and the sea. He attributed its apparent speed to the camera tracking a point while the jet moves. https://www.metabunk.org/posts/220136/
  - **Official convergence:** NASA (2023) and AARO (2025) reached the same altitude and wind-drift conclusion. AARO formally attributes the apparent speed to parallax (see section 3).
- **West on GIMBAL (2026):** The glare explanation is still debated. In April 2026, West called the glare theory "very well demonstrated" but acknowledged the initial rotation is unexplained. Critics argue that the object itself rotates. https://www.metabunk.org/threads/the-gimbal-ufo-marik-claims-new-findings-falsify-prosaic-explanations.14839/
  - **Official status:** No government body has endorsed or rejected the GIMBAL glare theory. https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/UAP-Case-Resolution-Reports
- **Criticism of AARO's GoFast work:** Critics argue AARO did not interview the pilots or estimate the object's size. https://www.dailygrail.com/2025/02/not-so-gofast-uap-government-office-presents-analysis-of-navy-video/

## 6. Timeline, 2004 to 2026
- **14 Nov 2004:** Nimitz encounter. FLIR1 recorded. https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/tic-tac-ufo-sighting-uap-video-dave-fravor-alex-dietrich-navy-fighter-pilots-house-testimony
- **2007:** FLIR video posted online without authorization. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf
- **Aug–Sept 2008:**
  - The DIA "AAWSAP" solicitation was posted.
  - BAASS (Bigelow) was awarded the contract on 22 Sept 2008.
  - Elizondo's slides say AAWSAP was renamed AATIP, which The Black Vault disputes.
  - Source: https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-black-vaults-post-2017-uap-ufo-timeline-project/
- **Program funding:** According to the NYT (2017), the program, pushed by Sen. Harry Reid, cost **$22 million**. Politico says Reid secured the appropriation in 2009. **Note:** the commonly cited "2007 start" comes from press accounts, while FOIA records show the contract was awarded in 2008. https://seclists.org/interesting-people/2017/Dec/57

## Contested: do not state as fact
- That the objects were extraterrestrial or "non-human." No official body has found this, and AARO says there is no verifiable evidence. https://rollcall.com/2024/03/08/no-evidence-of-extraterrestrial-technology-report-finds/
- That the 2004 "Executive Summary" is an official Pentagon or Navy document. Its provenance is unverified. https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-vault-files-the-tic-tac-incident-november-14-2004/
- **Fravor's account:** the object's size (about 40 ft), its acceleration, "disappearing," and its awareness of the jets. Attribute all of it to Fravor. https://www.bangordailynews.com/2017/12/19/news/former-navy-pilot-describes-encounter-with-ufo-studied-by-secret-pentagon-program/
- The radar descent "from 80,000 ft" and the two weeks of tracking by USS Princeton. This is Fravor's account, and the radar data has not been released. https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/David-Fravor-Statement-for-House-Oversight-Committee.pdf
- **Graves:** the "cube in a sphere" (second-hand) and the claim of daily sightings. Attribute these to Graves and the NYT. https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Ryan-HOC-Testimony.pdf
- Grusch's crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering claims. He says he was told of them, and AARO and DoD reject them. https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Dave_G_HOC_Speech_FINAL_For_Trans.pdf
- That AATIP was a "UFO program," that Elizondo directed it, and that AAWSAP was renamed AATIP. DoD disputes all three. https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/21-F-1458.pdf ; https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-black-vaults-post-2017-uap-ufo-timeline-project/
- That the videos were "declassified." DoD called them **unclassified** videos authorized for release. Say "officially released." https://www.theregister.com/2020/04/27/dod_ufo_video/
- That GIMBAL is glare and FLIR1 is a distant plane. These are Mick West's theories, not official findings. https://www.leonarddavid.com/?p=23728
- That GOFAST was "debunked." AARO resolved it as a non-anomalous object at about 13,000 ft moving with the wind (likely a balloon or similar), but did not identify the object. Say "AARO's analysis concluded…". https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/pentagon-releases-aaro-report-on-go-fast-video-analysis/
- **Wrong GOFAST year:** Some outlets (Fox News, Space.com) date it to 2016. AARO lists it as January 2015. https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/UAP-Case-Resolution-Reports
- The 21 January 2015 date for both GIMBAL and GOFAST. It comes from a Navy response to The Black Vault, not the DoD statement, so attribute it. https://sofrep.com/news/us-navy-reveals-the-truth-about-two-infamous-ufo-videos/
- PURSUE release counts. Sources disagree, so check war.gov/UFO. https://uap.global/learn/2026-uap-file-releases-timeline/
