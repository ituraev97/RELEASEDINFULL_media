What were the CIA's "Family Jewels"? In May 1973, CIA Director James Schlesinger ordered every employee to report anything the agency had done that might be outside its legal charter. The answers became a secret file, now declassified, of about 700 pages: Project Mockingbird wiretaps on reporters, the New York mail intercept, Operation CHAOS, help for E. Howard Hunt before Watergate, and a member of the Mafia used in an attempt to assassinate Fidel Castro. We read the file itself, the Rockefeller Commission's 1975 report, and the State Department's record of what happened when the story leaked in December 1974.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
