## 4. Nov 18-19 2025 - H.R. 4405 / Public Law 119-38
- House 427-1 on Tue Nov 18 2025; lone no: Rep. Clay Higgins (R-La.) (said bill "abandons 250 years of criminal justice procedure"). Johnson brought it up under suspension (2/3 needed) rather than waiting for petition. [AP via WHYY; NPR]
  - Johnson: "This is a raw and obvious political exercise," but voted yes; urged Senate to amend to protect "victims and whistleblowers"; Thune declined.
  - Massie (R): "they are finally on the side of justice" [NPR/Roll Call].
  - Khanna (D) warned against senators changes that would "muck it up".
- Senate passed by unanimous consent hours later, no objection, no roll call. Schumer (D, Minority Leader): "This is about giving the American people the transparency they've been crying for." (AP) and "This isn't about Democrats versus Republicans, or about Congress versus the president" (Roll Call). NPR attributes the UC request to a Schumer motion; CBS says it does not say who requested -> DISCREPANCY on who sought UC. Thune (R, Majority Leader): "you got a 427 to 1 vote, it's probably not likely to happen" (CBS).
- Signing: Trump announced evening Wed Nov 19 2025 he had signed; Roll Call gives bill number H.R. 4405 and 30-day deadline. Trump on Truth Social: "This latest Hoax will backfire on the Democrats just as all of the rest have." Massie's office cites it as Public Law 119-38.
