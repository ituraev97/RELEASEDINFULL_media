On June 25, 2021, U.S. intelligence gave Congress its first public assessment of unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP): 144 military reports from 2004 to 2021. It could identify one of them with high confidence: "a large, deflating balloon." We read the nine-page ODNI report line by line — the 18 incidents with "unusual flight characteristics," the 11 near misses, the five possible explanations, the stigma that kept pilots quiet — then what came next: AARO, 510 reports, 163 balloon-like objects, the 2023 balloon off South Carolina, and the Navy task force videos released in 2026.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
