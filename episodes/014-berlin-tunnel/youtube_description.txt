What was the Berlin Tunnel? In 1954 and 1955 the CIA and British intelligence (MI6) secretly dug a 1,476-foot spy tunnel from a cover warehouse in West Berlin under the border into Soviet East Berlin, and tapped Soviet and East German telephone cables. But the Soviets already knew: George Blake, a KGB mole inside MI6, had told them about the tunnel before the digging began.
We read the CIA's own declassified history of the operation (Clandestine Services History No. 150, written in 1968): the "dig a hole and put the dirt in it" warehouse, the baseball game at the border, the secret tap chamber under the road to Schoenefeld, 50,000 reels of tape and 368,000 transcribed conversations, and the night of April 22, 1956, when a hidden microphone recorded Soviet and East German crews discovering the tap. Also known as Operation Gold.
