## Blake date handling (assessment requested)

However, line 61 shows the t_blake crop, which visibly reads "In April 1956, MI-6 discovered that George BLAKE…", and nothing in the script addresses the discrepancy. The cold open (line 5, focus 0.7) and line 62 (focus 0.75) probably frame the lower lines, but this should be checked.
