What happened over Washington in July 1952? On July 19–20 and again on July 27, 1952, radar at Washington National Airport and Andrews Air Force Base tracked mysterious blips, the Air Force scrambled interceptors, and the 1952 Washington UFO sightings made headlines across the country. We read the declassified files: Project Blue Book's own July 1952 chart, the CIA memos that followed ("distinguish hardware from phantom"), the 1953 Robertson Panel report, and the Tremonton, Utah UFO film of July 2, 1952, a digitized print of which the Pentagon published in September 2026, together with a 1952 recording of Captain Edward J. Ruppelt.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
