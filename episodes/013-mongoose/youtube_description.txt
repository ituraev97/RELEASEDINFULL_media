What was Operation Mongoose? It was the Kennedy administration's secret war on Fidel Castro's Cuba, run by Gen. Edward Lansdale and the CIA's Task Force W, with a target date for "open revolt and overthrow" in the first two weeks of October 1962, the month of the Cuban Missile Crisis.
We read the declassified record: President Kennedy's November 30, 1961 order, Robert Kennedy's "no time, money, effort, or manpower is to be spared", Lansdale's 32 tasks and six-phase timetable, the 2-cent "Operation Bounty" idea, the "Elimination by Illumination" testimony, the CIA's Miami station JM/WAVE, NSAM 181 ("Mongoose Plan B plus"), the October 1962 stand-down, and what the Church Committee found in 1975.

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
