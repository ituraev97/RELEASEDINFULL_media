In March 1968 a Soviet ballistic missile submarine sank in the Pacific with all hands, about 16,500 feet down. Six years later, the CIA went to get it, with a ship presented to the world as a Howard Hughes deep-ocean mining vessel: the Hughes Glomar Explorer. We read the CIA's own history of Project AZORIAN (released in 2010, with whole pages still blank), the White House meeting of March 19, 1975, the President's Daily Brief that said the ship "raised part of their submarine," the Soviet ship that radioed "What are you doing here?" — and the court case that gave us the Glomar response: "neither confirm nor deny."

Facts come from documents and official sources. Claims are attributed. Theories are labelled as theories.
