How did a 60-count draft federal indictment against Jeffrey Epstein end in 13 months in a Florida county jail, with work release for up to 12 hours a day, six days a week? We read the Justice Department's own review of the 2007 Epstein non-prosecution agreement (the Epstein plea deal), the record the House Oversight Committee and Congress are fighting over again in November 2026.
Inside: the 60-count draft, the "any potential co-conspirators" clause, the "Florida Science Foundation" work release, the victims' lawsuit, the 2019 New York charges, the DOJ's 2020 "poor judgment" finding and the 2026 fight over the Epstein files.

Part 1 of our series on the Epstein records. Facts come from official records and court documents; reporting is attributed. Claims about people are limited to what the records say.
