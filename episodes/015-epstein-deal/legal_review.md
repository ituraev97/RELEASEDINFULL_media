## Overall verdict

The script is careful and almost all of it traces to the record. It still needs **27 FIXes** before publishing. The most serious are:
1. **Document crops (item C1).** The crops have not been made yet (`/home/claude/src/img/doc/` is empty). The pages they come from name line prosecutors, defense lawyers and state officials. Several also hold dangerous material directly next to the quoted lines: a prosecutor's email accusing the State Attorney of "scheming", a passage about Alan Dershowitz, the Bear Stearns cooperation allegation, explicit evidence lists, and indictment paragraphs that describe sex acts.
2. **"60 counts" framing (title card, close, thumbnail C).** The 60-count indictment was only ever a draft. It was never approved or filed.
3. **Omissions that are unfair to identifiable people**, including the co-conspirator clause's origin and the supervisor's explanation.
4. **Acosta's 2019 claim about the State Attorney** runs without the State Attorney's public denial.
5. **Rule 3:** the Maxwell acquittal on one count and the dismissal of Epstein's 2019 indictment are both missing.
6. **Insinuating lines:** "under that kind of watch", "Every secret has a release date", "Whether that is enough is the question Congress is still voting on".
