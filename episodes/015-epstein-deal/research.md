  - PDF p.4 (ii): NPA terms (plead to pending state indictment + procurement of minors for prostitution F.S. 796.03; binding recommendation 18 months county jail + 12 months community control; damages provisions; USAO forgoes federal prosecution in SDFL of Epstein, four named co-conspirators and "any potential co-conspirators"); "Victims were not informed of, or consulted about..."; nine months of defense efforts, "extensive team of prominent attorneys", "quintessentially state matter"; June 23, 2008 ODAG would not intervene; June 30, 2008 plea; consecutive 12 + 6 months, 12 months community control; began serving that day in minimum-security Palm Beach County facility; July 7, 2008 CVRA petition (Jane Doe; later Jane Doe 2).
