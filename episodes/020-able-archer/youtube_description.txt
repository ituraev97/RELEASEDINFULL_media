In 1983 a routine NATO drill may have looked to some in Moscow like cover for a nuclear strike. A once-secret report to the President says the Soviets were "genuinely worried", and its own authors admit the depth of that fear is hard to gauge.
We read the declassified paper trail: the exercise report, the KGB's warning effort, the memo that reached President Reagan, and the argument inside U.S. intelligence over whether the war scare was real.

Question for you: was Able Archer a near miss, or a scare that grew in the telling? The documents point both ways. Tell us in the comments which reading you think the records support, and please keep it to the records.
