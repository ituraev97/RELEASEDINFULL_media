# RELEASED IN FULL — media & pipeline

Independent documentary channel about declassified and officially released U.S. government files. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any U.S. government agency.

- `pipeline/` — narration (Kokoro), graphics (Playwright), edit (frame-exact 30 fps), synthesized score & SFX, QC, Shorts, thumbnails.
- `brand/` — logo, avatar, banner, watermark, brand sheet.
- `episodes/` — scripts, research, fact-checks, rights logs. No video on `main`.
- `topics.md` — season 1 slate with sources. `RUNBOOK.md` — how to produce an episode.
- `drop-mon` … `drop-sun` — rotating upload slots for Metricool (one day's files, force-pushed).

Footage: U.S. government works (public domain) via NAVAIR FOIA and DVIDS. Appearance of U.S. government visual information does not imply or constitute endorsement.
