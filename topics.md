# RELEASED IN FULL — Season 1 Topic Slate (FILE 001–030)

Prepared 2026-10-29. US audience. Document-led, public-domain-first.

## How to read this

**URL tags**
- **[OPENED]**: fetched this session and checked against its title or content.
- **[LINKED]**: the direct link appears on an [OPENED] page, but I did not fetch it separately (some agency hosts block automated fetches).
- **[SEARCH]**: came back in web search results, but I could not open it. Check it by hand before citing.

**Things that affect the whole slate**
- **CIA reading room is down.** On 2026-10-29, every `cia.gov/readingroom/...` URL returned a "System maintenance" page. CIA reading-room links below are tagged [SEARCH]. For each one, an [OPENED] mirror of the same government document is given where one exists (National Security Archive / GWU, Ford Library, Senate). `cia.gov/resources/csi/...` pages did open.
- **Mirrors are copies, not the source.** NSArchive (gwu.edu) and The Black Vault host copies of US-government documents. The documents themselves are public domain, but the sites' own commentary and layout are not. On screen, credit the originating agency and use the agency copy where possible.
- **Check each NARA item before using it.** NARA catalog IDs below come from NARA's own topic pages ([OPENED]). Each item still needs a check that it is digitized and has no use restrictions. Newsreels such as Paramount and Universal can carry rights limits even when NARA holds them.
- **Check for newer UAP releases.** AARO and NARA's UAP collection (RG 615) keep adding material. Look for anything released after Nov 2024 before scripting the UAP episodes.

**Fact risk**
- **Low:** the documents tell the story directly.
- **Med:** the core is documented, but popular versions overreach or the interpretation is disputed.
- **High:** the story rests mostly on contested claims.

**Season arc**
- 11 of the 30 episodes are UFO/UAP. Four are modern UAP (001, 008, 020, 030) and seven are historical UFO files.
- UFO/UAP episodes alternate with Cold War files, so the channel never runs two UFO episodes back to back.
- The strongest hooks (Northwoods, Area 51, MKUltra, Roswell, the missile photos) come in the first six episodes.
- The season ends with "what the government says now."

## FILE 002 — Operation Northwoods: The Pretext Memo
- **Working title:** "The Joint Chiefs Signed It: Operation Northwoods"
- **Hook:** In 1962 the Joint Chiefs sent the Defense Secretary a list of staged incidents that could justify invading Cuba, and it was rejected.
- **Primary sources:**
  - [OPENED] NSArchive posting, "Pentagon Proposed Pretexts for Cuba Invasion in 1962" (2001), which links the 15-page JCS memo "Justification for US Military Intervention in Cuba," 13 Mar 1962: https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/news/20010430/
  - [LINKED] Memo PDF: https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/news/20010430/northwoods.pdf
  - [OPENED] NARA JFK Collection copy, record no. 202-10002-10104 (JCS Central Files 1962, Box 29), released via a NARA FOIA letter and mirrored: https://documents3.theblackvault.com/documents/jfkfiles/northwoods-nara.pdf
  - [OPENED] FRUS 1961–63 vol. X (Cuba), Lansdale's "Institutional Planning, Operation Mongoose," 13 Mar 1962. Same day, same policy context: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v10-12mSupp/d280
- **Visuals:** The memo pages are the star. Add NARA/JFK Library photos of the Joint Chiefs and Lemnitzer, and Cuban Missile Crisis reconnaissance photos for context (see 006). Animated document highlights work well.
- **Sensitivity:** Advertiser-safe if framed as history. Do not tie it to 9/11 or other "false flag" claims; that framing invites a misinformation label and is not in the documents. Everyone named is deceased.
- **Fact risk:** **Low** for the memo. **Med** for anything about intent beyond the text. Kennedy and McNamara rejected it; say so.
